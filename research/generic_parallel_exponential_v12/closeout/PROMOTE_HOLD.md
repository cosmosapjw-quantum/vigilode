# G2 PROMOTE / HOLD

## PROMOTE

- projected Padé13 exponential and dense φ-action reference;
- counted strictly matrix-free full-Arnoldi φ-action;
- EXPRB2 and EXPRB43 correctness foundation;
- published `pexprb54s4` coefficients and selected order identities;
- actual three-level local-Rayon dependency execution;
- nonlinear 2/4/5 observed-order gate;
- stiff-linear and damped-oscillatory linear gate;
- 100-digit independent verification;
- G0 scope lock and frozen BDF/Radau comparators.

## HOLD

- KIOPS label or production φ residual bound;
- adaptive exponential solver;
- same-error performance promotion;
- nonautonomous, nonidentity-mass, sparse, DAE, MPI/GPU claims;
- automatic EPIRK-K/W dispatch;
- physical-client execution.

## Decision

```text
G2_FOUNDATION = COMPLETE / PROMOTE
G2_GENERIC_ACCELERATOR = HOLD
PERFORMANCE_PROMOTION_AUTHORIZED = false
```

The foundation is sufficient to open the fused/adaptive φ-action node. It is not sufficient to rank exponential methods against protected JF RODAS5P.
