# G2 literature source map

1. V. T. Luan and A. Ostermann, “Parallel exponential Rosenbrock methods,” Computers & Mathematics with Applications 71 (2016) 1137–1150, DOI `10.1016/j.camwa.2016.01.020`.
   - `pexprb54s4` coefficient authority;
   - order-5/embedded-4 pair;
   - parallel dependency structure and stiff order conditions.

2. M. Hochbruck, A. Ostermann, and J. Schweitzer, “Exponential Rosenbrock-Type Methods,” SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 47 (2009), DOI `10.1137/080717717`.
   - general exponential Rosenbrock formulation and stiff convergence context.

3. S. Gaudreault and J. A. Pudykiewicz / KIOPS implementation literature: “KIOPS: A fast adaptive Krylov subspace solver for exponential integrators,” J. Comput. Phys. 357 (2018/2019 literature trail), arXiv `1804.05126`.
   - fused linear combinations of φ-functions;
   - incomplete orthogonalization;
   - adaptive Krylov parameters and substepping.

4. M. Narayanamurthi, P. Tranquilli, A. Sandu, and M. Tokman, “EPIRK-W and EPIRK-K time discretization methods,” J. Sci. Comput. 78 (2019), DOI `10.1007/s10915-018-0761-3`.
   - W/K order theories for approximate operators and Krylov projections.
