# Preregistration: component triage before the production solver (CT)

Registered on branch `claude/nice-dijkstra-208xq0`, fast-forwarded to the latest
backup `audit/production-readiness-20261018` at `da91553`. Authority inputs:
`DECISION.json` (A07 gate correction), `research/production_audit_20261018/
{DECISION,NEXT_STEPS,FINDINGS}.json`, `research/LEDGER.jsonl` up to L-0136.

## Question

Which of the solver's components should the production solver take as its
default (PROMOTE), offer behind a validated router predicate (SPECIALIZE), keep
but improve and re-measure first (OPTIMIZE), or drop from the production crates
(PRUNE, which includes relocation to a research crate)? Every component in
`COMPONENTS.json` receives exactly one label.

## Claim boundary

- This node classifies. It does not close any mathematical obligation
  (A01-A05, A11). A component with an open obligation carries
  `provisional = true` and `blocked_by`, and cannot be PROMOTE.
- Instructions (callgrind Ir), allocation events, allocated bytes and
  `WorkCounters` are separate units. No conversion between them.
- Wall time is diagnostic unless `timing_authority_registry` reports
  `Admissible` for this campaign's own (sessions, cases) design after a new
  `session-median-coverage-study` and an independent review. Until then a
  wall-time figure can only raise `TIMING_CONFLICT`, never decide a label.
- External solvers (Diffsol 0.16.1, SciPy, SUNDIALS CVODE/IDA, Hairer
  RADAU5/RODAS) are used for reference cross-checks and for the OPTIMIZE(b)
  external gap only. They never become an internal rival.
- No historical PASS/FAIL record is rewritten. Superseding statements go to new
  ledger rows.

## Components

`COMPONENTS.json` (generated by `build_manifests.py` at the registration
commit) lists 142 components in 11 families with their slot (choice point),
reach (D default path, O opt-in, R research-only, T corpus, S support), arms,
prior ledger evidence and blocking obligations. The arm-to-component mapping
is the one printed by `rodas5p triage-list`; the manifest's `arms` field is
refreshed from it before the run, never edited by hand.

## Corpus and sweep

`CORPUS.json` lists the problems with tier (G generic stiff, P sparse/PDE,
C physics client, V verified error), calibration/holdout partition and
reference kind. Sweep: rtol in {1e-3, ..., 1e-10}; output grids `endpoint` and
`uniform101`; initial step scale h0 in {0.5, 1, 2}. callgrind is limited to the
`profile_subset` of `CORPUS.json`; the full sweep is measured with
`WorkCounters` and accuracy.

The solver has no component-wise atol, so every arm, internal and external,
uses the scalar `atol = rtol * atol_scale` of the problem. The missing vector
atol is recorded as a product gap, not hidden.

## Measurements (contract: `HARNESS_CONTRACT.md`)

1. Rows: `rodas5p triage-run`, one process per cell, failures kept as typed
   rows (`solver_failure`, `refused`, `error`, `process_abort`).
2. Accuracy: independent references only. Exact solutions where they exist,
   otherwise two different codes on the output grid (SciPy Radau and LSODA at
   rtol 1e-13), radius `rho = 2 * max discrepancy`. A (cell, target) with
   `E_rho > 0.1 * target` is `reference_limited` and excluded. The solver is
   never its own reference.
3. Parity: f and the analytic Jacobian of every problem agree between Rust and
   the reference models to relative 1e-13 before any reference is used.
4. Cost: callgrind Ir, warm = Ir(2) - Ir(1), cold = Ir(1) - Ir(0), with
   Ir(1) == Ir(1b) required (determinism). Build profile `bench` only.
   Allocation: `rodas5p-allocation-audit triage`, one raw window per
   integration after warmups. Counters: `work-counters-v2-attempted`.
5. Wall (diagnostic): `tools/triage/host_prep.sh` record, then paired
   multi-process sessions (`paired_receipt.rs`) on the user's machine.

## Decision rules (thresholds: `THRESHOLDS.json`, frozen here)

0. Static reach: a research, gate, audit, receipt or corpus module with no
   runtime dependent is PRUNE (relocate). A runtime dependent found by
   `triage_manifest.py --static-reach` removes the static label and the module
   is measured (`g4_s5b0_inner_tolerance` and `raw_stage_target` are known
   runtime dependents and are measured under their component ids).
1. Correctness: C1 accuracy and C3 fail-closed failures without a repair path
   in scope give PRUNE; with a repair path and a unique capability, OPTIMIZE.
2. Frontier (per problem, grid, target): cost of the cheapest admissible rtol
   group (all three h0 scales accurate) divided by the rival's. Rival = the arm
   that differs only in this component's slot (attribution), else the cheapest
   applicable arm with another component of the slot (strongest). GAIN <= 0.98,
   NEUTRAL <= 1.02, LOSS otherwise; one-sided reachability is a capability
   gain or loss.
3. PROMOTE: GAIN/NEUTRAL on >= 90 % of applicable G and P cells, no ratio
   above 1.10, C2 (requested-tolerance ratio <= 10 on >= 95 % of groups and
   <= 100 on all), warm allocation 0 or a documented n-independent constant,
   not provisional.
4. SPECIALIZE: a non-empty win set described by a predicate on the declared
   features, found on calibration problems and confirmed on holdout problems
   (`tools/holdout_verdict.py`: >= 5 positives, stable under a one-rank
   threshold shift).
5. OPTIMIZE: a LOSS whose profile attribution leaves a headroom-adjusted ratio
   <= 1.02, or a unique capability with an external same-family counted-work
   gap > 1.5, or a unique capability whose win set is not characterizable.
6. PRUNE: everything else. Among NEUTRAL alternatives of one slot the larger
   one (LOC) is PRUNE.
7. A wall-time ratio that contradicts the Ir decision by more than 15 % with an
   interval excluding 1 sets `TIMING_CONFLICT` and `provisional`.

## Evidence reuse

A prior ledger verdict is reused only when (a) the component's source is
unchanged since that row, (b) it does not rest on an instrument found defective
(Diffsol `main.rs` harness, LP09/LP10 checkers, ME01 rival rule, allocation
audit warm `saturating_sub`, single-process stiff-benchmark wall time,
self-reference), and (c) the scope matches. `COMPONENTS.json` marks each prior
with `reuse` and the reason.

## Instrument controls (stop rule)

Before the main campaign the smoke run must reproduce all of:
1. SPD02 always-on column extents: Ir LOSS against the Legacy LU policy on
   the small G-tier problems (ledger L-0082 FAIL).
2. E8 `solve_gmres_into`: Ir GAIN against `solve_gmres` (ledger L-0045).
3. `DenseErrorControl::Off` on the nonlinear Prothero-Robinson problem with
   101 outputs, rtol 1e-8: requested-tolerance ratio far above 100 (audit:
   1801.6), so C2 flags it.
4. A forged certified Leja cache: refused at compile time or run time (SA-01).

Any mismatch is treated as a harness defect and stops the campaign.

## Not claimed

No wall-time speedup, no global error guarantee, no production promotion of a
component with open obligations, no statement about solvers outside this
repository beyond the measured cells.

## Amendments before the main campaign (from the smoke run)

Amendments change instruments, never thresholds that decide labels, and each
one names the smoke observation that forced it.

1. **Ir determinism tolerance.** `mf` on oregonator at rtol 1e-8 gave
   Ir(1) = 846643399 and Ir(1b) = 846643395 (4 instructions in 8.5e8; the
   output states were bitwise identical). Exact equality would void such a
   cell. `THRESHOLDS.profile.ir_determinism_relative_tolerance = 1e-6` now
   accepts a relative gap at or below 1e-6, three orders below the 2 % LOSS
   margin. Larger gaps still void the cost (`callgrind_nondeterministic`).
2. **Attribution counterpart.** A counterpart arm is any arm equal outside the
   component's slot and without the component (`tools/triage/triage_check.py`
   `counterparts`), because the fast driver's Legacy LU policy holds three
   slot members at once and opt-in features compare against the arm without
   them.
3. **Independent harness review (before any decisive run).** A read-and-run
   review of the CLI found, and the harness now fixes:
   - `triage-profile-run` measured result merging, the in-process determinism
     comparison and result drops inside Ir(2) - Ir(1): 8.6 % (fast-small,
     robertson) and 6.2 % (fast, scalar-linear) of warm Ir, which pulls ratios
     toward 1. Now 0.065 % / 0.066 %. Every Ir figure taken before this fix
     (the smoke controls C1 and C2) is re-measured.
   - Two arms (`seq-mf-gmres-x0-prevstep*`) could never run and would have
     produced false PRUNE and capability-gain evidence; they are unwired and
     their components carry coverage waivers.
   - BDF and Radau arms of different order shared component sets; order and
     stage components (`bdf.order*`, `radau.stages*`) now separate them, and
     `radau5-tier-a`'s second knob is its own component
     (`radau.reuse_stage_lu`) with one-knob arms `radau5-scaled` and
     `radau5-reuse-lu`. No two wired arms share a component set (tested).
   - Error rows carry `null` counters instead of zeros; `refused` is limited to
     identified pre-work validations; problems carry `autonomous`.
4. **Row size.** The smoke rows (G tier only) took 156 MB because every row
   stores all 101 output states. A P-tier row (n up to 8192) would take about
   16 MB, so the main campaign would need hundreds of GB. Rows with dimension
   above 64 now keep their states in a verified gzip sidecar
   (`<sha256>.f64le.gz`) and carry `accuracy_inline`, computed by the same
   `triage_common.row_accuracy` function the checker uses and bound to the
   SHA-256 of its reference entry. The checker recomputes from inline states
   or a verified sidecar whenever available and records `accuracy_source` for
   every row; inline values are used only as the documented fallback. The
   accuracy rule and thresholds are unchanged.
