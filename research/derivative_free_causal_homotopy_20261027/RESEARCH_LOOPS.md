## Remaining theory versus engineering

The exact physical quotient is established only for its declared model family.
Generalization needs closure-defect estimates and output error transport.
Changing the carrier of a Rosenbrock formula needs its own order conditions.
The cubic monotonicity/nonexpansivity proof does not certify arbitrary RHS
maps, and intermediate homotopy stations do not cure a final noncontractive
target by themselves. These are mathematical obligations.

The existing adapter pressure/backoff/return rules and proxy cost weights
remain policy/measurement obligations. The new strict comparison solves the
observed same-window choice, not optimal future exploration. Structural
`CoreResult` failures still lack a partial-work object; integrate that API into
an adaptive failed-attempt fallback only after adding a charged error return.
The shared tableau's one fixed 8x8 metadata inverse is disclosed separately
from the zero per-step/state-matrix count. These are implementation contracts,
not defects in the proved model-specific reduction.
