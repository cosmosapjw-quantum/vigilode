Evaluate each affine factor in original listed state-term order, product in original listed factor order, then derivative of each factor by product of all other factors, again in original order, accumulating time and state columns exactly as the Vec evaluator. Duplicate indices and repeated factors are not combined. Workspace only contains staging scratch, not source authority or stale results; every requested element overwritten on success.
