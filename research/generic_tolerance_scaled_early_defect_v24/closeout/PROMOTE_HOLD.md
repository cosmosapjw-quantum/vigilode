# P1-00 promote / hold

## Promote

- `rho_c2_wrms` as a read-only, tolerance-aware early-flow-defect feature;
- entry-window geometry as a predeclared diagnostic view;
- a successor **read-only event-conditioned shadow** experiment.

## Hold

- any selected numerical threshold;
- blanket all-attempt admission;
- event trigger inferred from `rho` alone;
- early abort in the committed solver;
- controller/cache transfer;
- active method switching;
- polyalgorithm speedup claim.

## Reason

Scaling restores lost tolerance information and materially improves the
entry-window conservative frontier, but it does not determine when an R-JF
trajectory should pay for an E-K prefix and it does not provide perfect
classification.
