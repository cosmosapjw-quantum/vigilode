# Hypothesis Space

1. Read audited evidence and current scope.
2. Generate substantively distinct families, including conservative, mechanism, representation, regime, and null/systematic alternatives.
3. Merge relabelings.
4. Record assumptions, mechanism, limits, predictions, competitors, discriminator, and fatal vulnerability.
5. Update `state/HYPOTHESIS_GRAPH.md`.
