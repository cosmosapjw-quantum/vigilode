# Claim–Source Audit

1. Work independently from the synthesis author.
2. Inspect each claim–citation pair for exact support, conditions, scope, and conflicts.
3. Assign `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, `CONTESTED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `MISATTRIBUTED`, or `INFERENCE_ONLY`.
4. Update the evidence ledger.
5. Prevent unsupported claims from entering hypothesis generation as facts.
