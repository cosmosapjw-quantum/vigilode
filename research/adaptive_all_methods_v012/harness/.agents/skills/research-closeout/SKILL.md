# Research Closeout

1. Update all state artifacts.
2. Record confirmed, rejected, uncertain, and missing items.
3. Preserve negative results and what they rule out.
4. Compact the state to current evidence pointers, blockers, decisions, and next gate.
5. Do not preserve obsolete narrative merely because it was produced earlier.
