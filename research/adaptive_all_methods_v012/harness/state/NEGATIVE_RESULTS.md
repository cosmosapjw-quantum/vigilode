# Negative Results / Prohibited Shortcuts

- Same raw tolerance is not a fair cross-family comparison.
- Fixed-step BDF2 coefficients cannot be used after arbitrary step changes.
- One controller exponent cannot be assigned to every method.
- Step doubling is not free and is not automatically a production winner.
- Rejected work cannot be deleted from the ledger.
- Homotopy output correction cannot be treated as a rigorous error bound.
