## Closed checkpoint and description findings

**IR-03 — terminal step-proposal semantics (initially FIX_SOON; closed).** The original carry path made every batch target the native session's final time, where DOPRI exits before updating its proposal from the final accepted error. With `y'=1`, `h0=.1`, and one accepted step to `.1`, the exported request was `.1` when `tf=.1`, versus `.5` when `tf=1` and `.1` was an intermediate observation. The repair uses full-solve session endpoints and pauses at intermediate batch targets; the final solve endpoint exports no stale proposal. Selective review caught one boundary of that repair: native `AttemptBudget` can coincide with successful arrival at an intermediate target. The completed-target condition now treats that as successful batch arrival while preserving the global budget. Existing and independent one-attempt regressions pass; a global budget of one still stops the overall solve at `.1`.

No tolerance or numerical acceptance was changed.
