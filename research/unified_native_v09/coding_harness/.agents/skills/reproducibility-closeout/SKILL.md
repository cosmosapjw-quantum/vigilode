# Reproducibility Closeout

1. Collect branch/commit/worktree, environment, commands, seeds, data/artifact paths, and validation results.
2. Record failed approaches and what they rule out.
3. Decide `PROMOTE`, `HOLD`, `REWORK`, or `REVERT` from explicit gates.
4. Update `PLANS.md`, `RUN_STATE.md`, `DECISION_LOG.md`, `FAILURE_LOG.md`, and `VALIDATION_MATRIX.md`.
5. Leave a compact next-action state.
