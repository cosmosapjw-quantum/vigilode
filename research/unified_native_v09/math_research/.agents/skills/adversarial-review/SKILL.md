# Independent Adversarial Review

1. Do not optimize the candidate; try to prevent premature promotion.
2. Review evidence, logic, physical consistency, novelty, assumption burden, robustness, alternatives, and tractability separately.
3. Check for elegance-as-evidence, fitting-as-explanation, strawman alternatives, and narrative outrunning evidence.
4. Assign a role: `DEFENDED`, `TRADEOFF`, `INFORMATIVE_FAILURE`, `REJECTED_SHORTCUT`, or `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE`.
