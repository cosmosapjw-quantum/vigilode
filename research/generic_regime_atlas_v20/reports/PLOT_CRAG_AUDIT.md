## Correctness

mostly with E-K local inadmissibility, not with marginal wall differences. The residence plot

systems intentionally change their equations. The plots must therefore be read as generic regime
telemetry, not published benchmark work-precision diagrams.

## Generation

long E-K intervals justify one entry trial, while short/uncertain intervals should abstain to R-JF.
They also predict that an E-first transactional trial may be competitive on this atlas, but the old
oscillatory-PR/quadratic corpus must be included before adopting that policy.

- **Surviving:** persistent E-K/R-JF complementarity and selector information gain.
- **Narrowed:** the complementarity is mostly E-K admissibility vs R-JF robustness, not symmetric cost competition.
- **Rejected:** current plots prove a production switcher or universal E-K superiority.
