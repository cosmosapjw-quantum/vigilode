2. WHICH CODE FORMS THE TRUE STAGE RESIDUAL AND WHAT ITS THRESHOLD IS

   The true stage residual of the shipped driver is r = rhs_i - (I - h gamma J) U_i in the 2-norm, formed by
   true_residual_into (common.rs:104-120) with one application of ShiftedOperator through apply_counted, at
   gmres_into.rs:282-290 (top of a pass, Krylov category) and again at :375-383 (final, Diagnostic category).
   Threshold: tau = max(linear_atol, rtol_lin * ||rhs_i||_2) with rtol_lin = 1e-10 and
   linear_atol = gamma * 1e-14 rounded down (gmres_into.rs:269, common.rs:39-47,
   rodas5p_matrix_free_fast.rs:2787-2791). An iterate is returned iff ||r||_2 <= tau and finite
   (:301, :386); otherwise the solve is an error: budget exhaustion (:307-312), residual breakdown (:353-357),
   or "true residual exceeds" (:386-390). The driver turns CoreError::LinearSolve into a failed attempt
   (failure_kind, rodas5p_matrix_free_fast.rs:3241-3248).
   The independent audit of node V17 (arm A) rebuilds rhs_i and r_i from the stage equations with its own
   RHS and JVP and applies the same tau (mf_stage_solve/PREDECLARED.json, residual_check.A); it is reused.
