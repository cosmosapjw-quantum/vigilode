- Decision taken in the change (entry 1 left it open): a client whose first-use check is MultiColouring or
  MultiColouringPooled builds the further colourings in AssemblyStageClient::new (Engine::prepare_multi, now

  change. A requested MC check (handle, verify) still builds them at its first use.
