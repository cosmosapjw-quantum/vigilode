PROOF OF THE CLAIM (PREDECLARED mc_construction.claim_to_be_tested), in the precise form it holds
Setting: declared pattern P; client colouring c_0 (CPR) and further colourings c_1 .. c_R, all valid for P (two
declared columns of one row never share a group) with injective tuples j -> (c_0(j), .., c_R(j)). The
right-hand side is pure and its row i reads only the columns T_i: f_i(z) = f_i(z') when z, z' agree on T_i.
(i, k) is an undeclared dependency (k in T_i, k not in P_i) and the only one of row i (true in every wrong
declaration of the corpus; the parser checks it). Write S(G) = y + sum_{j in G} delta_j e_j, f0 = f(y),
and E_j(G) = (f_i(S(G)) - f0_i) / delta_j in floating point (the entry formula of the build).
(a) Build: either the c_0 group of k holds no declared column of row i, and the uncovered-row test of the
    build compares f_i(S({k})) with f0_i; or it holds exactly one, j0 (validity), and the assembled entry is
    A(i, j0) = E_j0({j0, k}) while every other declared entry of row i is clean, A(i, j) = E_j({j}).
(b) Further colouring r: either the group of k holds no declared column of row i, and the uncovered-row test
    of MC compares f_i(S({k})) with f0_i; or it holds exactly one, j_r, and MC recomputes
    R_r(i, j_r) = E_jr({j_r, k}) and R_r(i, j) = E_j({j}) for the other declared j of row i.
(c) If j0 exists, injectivity gives an r >= 1 with c_r(j0) != c_r(k), so R_r(i, j0) = E_j0({j0}) is compared
    with A(i, j0) = E_j0({j0, k}).
So MC detects (i, k) if and only if (U) some colouring, c_0 included, leaves row i uncovered in the group of
k and f_i(S({k})) != f0_i; or (X) E_j0({j0}) != E_j0({j0, k}); or (Y) for a partner j_r != j0,
E_jr({j_r, k}) != E_jr({j_r}) (compared with the clean A(i, j_r): c_0(j_r) != c_0(j0) = c_0(k)).
FA detects (i, k) if and only if f_i(S({k})) != f0_i (column k alone: row i is undeclared there, or the
build's uncovered-row test) or (X) (column j0 alone against A(i, j0)).
Consequences. MC performs FA's comparison (X) whenever j0 exists, and FA's raw comparison whenever some
colouring leaves row i uncovered. A case FA detects and MC misses therefore needs: every colouring pairs k
with a declared column of row i, f_i(S({k})) != f0_i, and every compared pair of entries equal. If the row
values themselves are equal at every combined state, f_i(S({j_r, k})) = f_i(S({j_r})) for every partner
(j0 included), the case is rounding-limited for MC by the pre-declared rule. The one remaining possibility
is an entry collapse: the row values differ at a combined state but the subtraction of f0_i and the
division by delta_j round both entries to the same number. That needs |d f_i / d y_k| delta_k at the level
of one ulp of f_i; it is not excused by the pre-declared rule and would count as a miss of MC (D fails).
The same collapse can hide (X) from FA. Symmetrically, a case MC detects and FA misses needs (Y) with (X)
and the raw comparison failing. Rows with several undeclared dependencies are outside this proof (their
contaminations can combine); the corpus has none. An impure right-hand side is caught because every
recomputed entry comes from a new call.
