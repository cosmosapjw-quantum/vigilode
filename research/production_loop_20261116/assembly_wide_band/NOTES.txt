HOW total_groups IS KNOWN AT CONSTRUCTION WITHOUT ANY RIGHT-HAND SIDE
- CheckColourings (assembly/multi.rs) is a function of the declared pattern alone: c1 = colour_columns(pattern)
  (pattern.rs:152-175, deterministic Curtis-Powell-Reid), the band (lower, upper) = pattern.bandwidth()
  (pattern.rs:98-108) and n = pattern.n() (CheckColourings::of, multi.rs:143-148). total of the band construction:
  sum of min(m_r, n) over band_moduli(lower + upper + 1, n) (multi.rs:94-106, :184-185); total of the refinement
  construction: sum over refinement_primes(largest c1 class) (multi.rs:109-121) of sum over the c1 classes of
  min(size, p) (:186-191); the construction with the smaller total is used, the band one on ties (:192-218), so
  total_groups (:268-270) = min(total band, total refinement). No t, no y, no right-hand side enters.
- The pattern is in ColumnRoute::Coloured(pattern), which AssemblyOptions::coloured(pattern) receives before any
  client exists; default_first_use_check(&route) can therefore decide from the pattern. Engine::prepare_multi
  (engine.rs:805-830) builds the same colourings from the client's colouring (Coloured::new: colour_columns of the
  same pattern, engine.rs:160, :212) and the band of the declared entries (engine.rs:816-821; equal to
  pattern.bandwidth()), so the number the default reads is the number of right-hand sides the MC check spends.
