## Addendum A3 (2026-11-17): node V19, frozen by this commit before any V19 run

Reason: in the V04 results the eight stage solves take 61-70% of the step in the six one-dimensional banded Brusselator cells (n = 1e3 to 1e5, lower = upper = 2; `cost_anatomy/AMDAHL.json`, fraction f_S), the only direct-solve regime where the stage chain and not the factorisation dominates, and no node of this loop addresses the solves of that regime (a level-structured tableau is bounded by 1.39 there). Reading of the protected kernel (`crates/rodas5p-integrators/src/rodas5p_fast.rs:528-558`, no measurement): both substitutions are scalar recurrences; in the back substitution the newest unknown enters the first subtraction and the row ends with a division, so every row waits for one multiplication, lower + upper subtractions and one division of the row before it, although the arithmetic of a row is small. No run of any arm of this node exists at the time of this commit. Contract: `V19_BANDED_SOLVE_KERNEL` in `TASK_CONTRACTS.json`. The node is run by the orchestrator session itself (no builder agent); it is covered by the final independent review of the branch.

Arms (all single-threaded, safe Rust, pivoted band LU with partial pivoting over rows k..k+lower as in the protected kernel):

- A0: the protected client `BandedStageLu` (Indexed kernel) through the `StageLinearSystem` trait.
- A1: a reference kernel inside the measurement program with the operation order of the protected kernel.
- B: sequential; factors bit-identical to A1 plus stored reciprocals of the pivots; forward substitution in the operation order of A1; back substitution in which the newest unknown enters last and the row ends with a multiplication by the reciprocal.
- C(P), P in {2, 4, 8}: the unknowns are split into P blocks of equal length separated by P - 1 separators of max(lower, upper) unknowns; pivoted band LU inside each block; explicit spikes (block inverse applied to the coupling columns); a dense reduced system on the separators with partial pivoting; the recurrences of the P blocks are executed in lockstep in one loop; inner back substitution as in B. A zero or non-finite pivot in a block or in the reduced system is returned as an error.
- BV, CV(P): B or C(P) with a check of the original system after every solve, counted in the cost: error unless max|b - W x| <= 1e-13 (norm_inf(W) norm_inf(x) + norm_inf(b)).

Validity suite, declared here (details and seeds in `banded_solve/PREDECLARED.json` before the first run): S1 the matrices W = I/(h gamma) - J of the V04 Brusselator band (lower = upper = 2) at n in {1e3, 1e4, 1e5}, two states, 1/(h gamma) in {1e6, 1e4, 1e2, 1, 1e-2, 1e-4, 1e-6}; S2 random strictly diagonally dominant bands, lower = upper in {1, 2, 4, 8}; S3 random bands without dominance, lower = upper in {1, 2, 4}; S4 constructed matrices that are well conditioned as a whole and have an exactly singular block interior for the partition of every P. eta = max|b - W x| / (norm_inf(W) norm_inf(x) + norm_inf(b)).
