0. Question
-----------
BoxKernel::solve (crates/vigilode/src/spatial/separable.rs) loads the real right-hand side as
complex data (imaginary part 0) and runs full complex FFTs along x, y and z, forward and inverse.
Half of that work transforms zeros and recomputes the conjugate half of a Hermitian spectrum.
Does pairing rows (THEORY.md 2.3 (ii), the involution already used by the sphere kernel) halve
the FFT work without changing the backward error (T1) or the stage mass identity (T2)?

1. Layout and notation
----------------------
Box cells nx, ny, nz (nz = 1 in 2-D), x fastest: index = x + nx (y + ny z).
A "row" is one x-line, r = y + ny z, rows = ny nz.  Every axis has at least 2 cells.
hx = floor(nx/2) + 1 = number of distinct x-modes of a real row (k = 0 .. floor(nx/2)).
N = nx ny nz.  F_x = DFT along x (length nx, e^{-2 pi i k x / nx}), likewise F_y, F_z.
Stage matrix in Fourier space: W(kx,ky,kz) = (z - c) + e_x(kx) + e_y(ky) + e_z(kz),
e_a(k) = rate_a 4 sin^2(pi k / n_a) (real, even: e_a(n_a - k) = e_a(k)).

2. Algebra of the pairing
-------------------------
Rows r0 = 2q and r0 + 1 (q = 0 .. floor(rows/2) - 1) are packed along x as one complex line
    w_q[x] = u[x] + i v[x],   u = b[row r0],  v = b[row r0 + 1]   (both real).
One complex FFT of length nx gives Z(m) = U(m) + i V(m) with U = F_x u, V = F_x v.
Real rows have Hermitian spectra, U(nx - m) = conj U(m) and V(nx - m) = conj V(m).
Define the involution (R Z)(m) = conj Z((nx - m) mod nx). Then R Z = U - i V, so
    U(m) = (Z(m) + (R Z)(m)) / 2,        V(m) = (Z(m) - (R Z)(m)) / (2 i),
for m = 0 .. hx - 1 only; the other modes are the conjugates.  (2i)^{-1} multiplies by -i/2:
(a + i b)(-i/2) = b/2 - i a/2, two exact halvings, no rounding beyond the subtraction.
At m = 0 (and m = nx/2 for even nx) the partner is the mode itself: U = Re Z, V = Im Z exactly,
so the DC and Nyquist entries of the half spectra are real by construction.
This is the same separation as SphereKernel::solve (THEORY.md 2.3 (ii), check F8 to 1e-12).

Inverse: after the y/z transforms, the division and the inverse y/z transforms, every row's
x-spectrum X_r(k), k < hx, is again the half of a Hermitian spectrum (the symbol W is real and
even in each axis, and the 3-D DFT of a real field satisfies F(-k) = conj F(k)).  Rebuild
    Z(m) = X_{r0}(m) + i X_{r0+1}(m)                   for m <  hx,
    Z(m) = conj X_{r0}(nx-m) + i conj X_{r0+1}(nx-m)    for m >= hx,
with the DC (and Nyquist) values projected to their real parts first (irfft semantics; their
imaginary parts are round-off and would otherwise leak from one row of the pair into the other).
One inverse complex FFT of length nx then returns u + i v: Re -> row r0, Im -> row r0 + 1,
both times 1/N.  Projection to the real part is what the Complex arm does at its very end
(`v.re * scale`), so both arms are the same linear map in exact arithmetic.

3. Where the Hermitian half is transformed along y and z
--------------------------------------------------------
The 3-D DFT of a real field is fixed by the planes kx = 0 .. floor(nx/2):
F(nx - kx, ky, kz) = conj F(kx, -ky, -kz).  So after the x stage only the half spectra
X_r(kx), kx < hx, are kept, in an x-mode-major buffer
    half[kx * rows + r] = half[kx * rows + z * ny + y]     (hx * rows complex values).
Along y and z these are genuinely complex data, so the y and z transforms are the existing full
complex plans of lengths ny and nz, applied to hx of the nx planes only:
  - y: for each (kx, z) the ny values are contiguous; one batched in-place call of the length-ny
    plan over the whole buffer (hx nz lines), no gather/scatter.
  - z (3-D only): for each kx the block half[kx*rows .. (kx+1)*rows] is an ny x nz array with
    y fastest; z-lines are gathered at stride ny inside that block (a compact block, unlike the
    Complex arm's stride nx ny over the whole field).
The separation pass writes this transposed layout directly (the two rows of a pair are
adjacent in it), and the rebuild pass reads it, so no extra transpose pass is needed.
Division: loop kx, z, y with the denominator in the same association as the Complex arm,
((z - c) + e_x(kx)) + e_y(y) + e_z(z), so the denominators are bit-identical, and no integer
division per element.

4. Odd sizes
------------
- Odd row count (rows = ny nz odd, i.e. ny and nz both odd): the last row is packed alone
  (v = 0) and separated with the same formula U = (Z + R Z)/2, i.e. projected onto the
  Hermitian subspace; its line costs one complex FFT for one real row.  No other change.
- Odd nx: hx = (nx + 1)/2, there is no Nyquist mode; (nx - m) mod nx pairs every m >= 1 with a
  distinct partner, m = 0 with itself.
- Even nx: the Nyquist mode m = nx/2 is its own partner and is real after separation.
- Pairs may straddle z planes when ny is odd (row (ny-1, z) with row (0, z+1)): harmless, the
  x stage acts row by row and the separated rows go to their own positions r.
- nx = 2, ny = 2, nz = 2 are covered (minimum sizes): hx = 2, partner of 1 is itself.

5. FFT work (complex transforms of a given length, one direction; both directions double it)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Complex arm:  x: rows lines of nx;  y: nx nz lines of ny;  z: nx ny lines of nz.
PairedReal:   x: ceil(rows/2) lines of nx;  y: hx nz lines of ny;  z: hx ny lines of nz.
At 256x256: 256 + 256 = 512 against 128 + 129 = 257 FFTs of length 256 per direction (0.502).
Extra O(N) passes: pack, separate, rebuild, split (about N/2 complex values each), against the
Complex arm's load and extract passes and its strided gather/scatter along y and z.
The divisions drop from N to hx rows (about N/2).

6. Plan reuse and memory
------------------------
Plans: the same rustfft complex plans (forward/inverse of lengths nx, ny, nz) serve both arms;
they are built once per kernel (BoxKernel::new: once per SeparableStages client = once per run,
once per solve_stage call) and reused by every solve.  No new plan kind, no new dependency.
Scratch: one in-place scratch of the largest plan requirement, shared by all transforms.
Memory (complex values of 16 bytes):
  Complex arm:  buffer N (+ line max(ny, nz) + scratch).
  PairedReal:   half hx * rows  (about N/2 + rows)
                + a batch of packed lines, batch * nx, batch = min(ceil(rows/2),
                  max(1, LINE_BATCH / nx)) with LINE_BATCH = 4096 complex (64 KiB): the x stage
                  streams through the rows in batches (pack -> FFT -> separate into half; and
                  rebuild -> inverse FFT -> split on the way back), so the packed lines never
                  exist for the whole field.  (+ line nz + scratch).
So PairedReal needs about half the memory of the Complex arm (256x256: 528 KiB + 64 KiB
against 1 MiB), and its working set during the y/z stages is the half buffer only.
Only the buffers of the selected arm are allocated.

7. API (design constraint of the task)
--------------------------------------
- `BoxTransform { Complex, PairedReal }` (non_exhaustive, Copy, Default = Complex) in
  separable.rs; `SeparableStages::with_box_transform(self, BoxTransform) -> Self` and
  `SeparableStages::box_transform()`.  The default stays Complex: only the owner-host timing
  cell may change the default (PLAN.json KS-N7 acceptance, orchestrator).
- The sphere ignores the setting: SphereKernel already pairs rings (2.3 (ii)).
- Constraint found: `vigilode::spatial` re-exports separable items in spatial.rs, which is
  outside KS-N7's allowed paths.  Until the orchestrator adds `BoxTransform` to that
  `pub use separable::{...}` line, the enum cannot be named outside the crate.  It therefore
  also implements FromStr ("complex", "paired-real", alias "paired") and Display, so callers and
  the tests select it with `.parse()` (type inferred from the setter), and the measurement test
  maps KS_N7_ARM directly.  Open issue for the merge: one-line re-export.
- `SeparableStages::stage_solver(z)`: a stage solver prepared once for z (plans, buffers and,
  on the sphere, the Thomas factors built once), returned as `impl FnMut(&mut [f64]) ->
  Result<(), SolveError> + Send` (no new nameable type needed).  It is what the protected driver
  does per attempt (one preparation, eight stage solves), and it is the measured object of the
  timing test.  `solve_stage` (kernel built per call) is unchanged.

8. Tests (append to crates/vigilode/tests/spatial_separable.rs; existing assertions unchanged)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Box grids of the existing tests: 8x6, 5x7, 4x6x5 (T1/T2), 4x5 (T4), 12x10 (T3).
Extra sizes for even/odd coverage: 2x2, 7x4, 6x9, 37x6 (prime nx), 3x3x3, 5x3x7, 2x5x3,
9x2x2, 16x12x4, 33x17, 64x48 (16 grids in all).
(z, c) range of the existing T1/T2 test: (0.5,0), (10,-1), (3,1), (1e4,0), (1e-3,0); seeds 0..3.
(a) Agreement with Complex.  Stated bound: on the box W = (z-c) I - L is an M-matrix with
    W 1 = (z - c) 1, hence ||W^{-1}||_inf = 1/(z - c), and ||W||_inf = (z - c) + rho_bar.
    Two solves whose residuals meet the T1 bound eta * S, S = ((z-c) + rho_bar)||x||_inf +
    ||b||_inf, differ by at most 2 eta S / (z - c).  Assert, with eta = 1e-13 (the T1 bound),
        ||x_paired - x_complex||_inf <= 1e-13 * S / (z - c),
    and print the largest relative difference ||x_p - x_c||_inf / ||x_c||_inf and the largest
    normalized difference ||x_p - x_c||_inf (z - c) / S.
(b) T1/T2 for PairedReal: backward error <= 1e-13 and stage mass defect <= 1e-13 with exactly
    the scales of separable_stage_solves_are_exact_and_mass_consistent, on the same grids and
    (z, c); plus right-hand sides whose paired rows differ in scale by 1e8 (the pairing mixes
    the two rows of a pair inside one FFT; the normwise bound must still hold).
(c) Client wiring: the T3 box case (12x10, c = -0.5) on the route with PairedReal: matches the
    dense matrix-exponential reference within the T3 envelope, mass defect <= 1e-12, and agrees
    with the Complex route within the same envelope.
(d) API: default Complex; parse/Display round trip; the sphere ignores the setting (identical
    bits); stage_solver gives the same bits as solve_stage.
