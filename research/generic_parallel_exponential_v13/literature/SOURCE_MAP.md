# G3/G4 literature source map

- Gaudreault, Rainwater, Tokman, KIOPS, JCP 372 (2018), DOI 10.1016/j.jcp.2018.06.026: fused phi combinations through one modified exponential, incomplete orthogonalization and adaptive Krylov parameters.
- Luan and Ostermann, Parallel exponential Rosenbrock methods, CAMWA 71 (2016), DOI 10.1016/j.camwa.2016.01.020: parallel order 4/5/6 ExpRB families and ideal critical-cost interpretation.
- Narayanamurthi et al., EPIRK-W and EPIRK-K, arXiv:1701.06528 / VT repository: separate order theories for arbitrary Jacobian approximations and Krylov-projected exact Jacobians.
- Deka and Einkemmer, Efficient adaptive step size control for exponential integrators, CAMWA 123 (2022), DOI 10.1016/j.camwa.2022.07.011: step cost is not constant when phi actions are iterative; cost-aware controller.
- Tokman, Loffeld, Tranquilli, New Adaptive EPIRK Methods, SIAM JSC, DOI 10.1137/110849961: EPIRK methods designed for adaptive Krylov evaluation.
