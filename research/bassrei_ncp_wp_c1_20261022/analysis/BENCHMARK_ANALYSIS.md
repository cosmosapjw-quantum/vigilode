# BASS/rei NCP C1: accuracy-matched work-precision benchmark — analysis for external review

This document is generated by `analysis/build_analysis.py` from the imported, ledger-bound results of
the campaign (ledger rows **L-0154**, attempt 2, and **L-0155**, attempt 3). The machine-readable data
are in `analysis/benchmark_summary.json`. The preregistration, with its amendments and the Results
section, is `PREREGISTRATION.md` in this directory, and it is normative.

## 1. What is and is not claimed

**Claimed [C]:**

- The VigilODE arms reach the application budget B_app = 1e-6 relative at the preregistered rung
  rtol 1e-8, on every preregistered case, with the source ledgers (B_src) held.
- Every comparator adapter evaluates the same right-hand side as the VigilODE kernels, to ≤ 3e-15.
- VigilODE results are bitwise identical across cores.
- VigilODE allocation does not depend on the step count, including forced rejections.
- Every preregistered item is accounted for, as OK or as a typed outcome.

**Not claimed:**

- Any speed claim. All timing is `diagnostic_only` under STATISTICAL_AUTHORITY_HOLD: one host, one
  campaign, no statistical authority. The matched-cost ratios below describe this run only.
- Production readiness, or a live BASS/rei integration. Both stay HOLD.

## 2. Setup (summary; the preregistration is normative)

**Host and execution.**

- An NCP host with 64 cores. The campaign was pinned to cores 8–63, with cores 0–7 left free.
- BLAS ran with 1 thread, under a 120-minute hard cap.
- Rust 1.94.1, locked and offline.
- scipy 1.11.4, Julia 1.12.7 (pinned Manifest), diffsol 0.17.1.

**Accuracy measure.**

- B_app = 1e-6 relative on {n_γ, ρ_γ, D_γ/ρ_γ (depletion-weighted, case-scaled floor), D_γ, x_HII,
  u, s/H} at every output time.
- B_src ledgers: 5e-12 per H for number and 5e-11 eV per H for energy (chemistry cases).
- References: dense LU at rtol 1e-12, cross-checked with Radau.

**Protocol.**

- Work-precision ladder: rtol 1e-3 … 1e-10.
- The **matched rung** of an arm is the loosest rtol at which it meets B_app and B_src.
- Cost pairs: AB/BA blocks, 2 warm-up and 7 confirmation blocks. The ratio is the median of the
  comparator's solve time over VigilODE's.

## 7. Interpretation (provisional; for the reviewer to check)

- **Stiff transport (S2):** VigilODE's structured client solver (P03) is the fastest arm at every grid.
  Its paired ratios against the strongest comparator, Julia CVODE_BDF Band, grow with the grid. The
  advantage comes from the exact energy-block solve, O(n), against a banded or sparse factorization.
- **Same method family:** VigilODE's banded RODAS5P against Julia's Rodas5P with an exact sparse
  Jacobian. Compare the matched-rung times in §5; they were not paired in AB/BA.
- **Bordered (S3):** the bordered structured client beats FBDF sparse, CVODE and VODE.
- **Stiff chemistry (S4S, S4R):** VigilODE dense and Julia CVODE_BDF KLU are within about 1.3× of each
  other, in both directions.
- **Non-stiff (S1, S4N1):** explicit Runge–Kutta methods (Julia Tsit5, diffsol Tsit45) are faster than
  every implicit method, VigilODE included, as expected.
- **The 2 TIMEOUTs** are DOP853 on the stiff chemistry case S4R. They are typed, with a 300 s cap.

## 8. Threats to validity

1. **Timing authority.** The results come from a single host and a single campaign, with no repeated
   campaigns or statistics (STATISTICAL_AUTHORITY_HOLD). The ratios can move with the host, the load
   and the build.
2. **Censoring.** Most VigilODE arms meet B_app already at the loosest rung (1e-3). The matched ratios
   are therefore bounded on VigilODE's side, not measured. It also means B_app does not discriminate
   among the VigilODE arms here; their rtol-1e-8 errors are 1e-11 to 1e-8.
3. **Jacobian asymmetry.**
   - VigilODE uses exact, structured Jacobians.
   - VODE MF22/MF25 and CVODE Band use difference-quotient Jacobians.
   - Julia's sparse arms use exact AD of a bitwise port.
   - diffsol uses the adapter's exact JVP or dense Jacobian.
4. **Problem size.** n ≤ 8322, and some arms are not applicable a priori (dense n ≤ 512 or 2048,
   diffsol without G4).
5. **Timing scope.** It differs by family; each row carries its `timing_scope`. Every runner
   integrates twice per call, a warm-up and then the measured call.
6. **The Julia environment was installed online** (Amendment 2). Its versions equal the pinned
   Manifest; the hashes are in `julia_install/ENV_SHA256SUMS.txt`.
7. **Non-blind design.** Pilots informed parts of the design; all of it is disclosed in the
   preregistration's design history and deviations.
