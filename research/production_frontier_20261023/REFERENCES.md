# Primary sources and reuse

Literature was discovered with Exa and SciSpace on2026-10-23. Results below are background; new implementation claims rest on the code, derivations and receipts in this package. SciSpace abstracts are discovery records, not proof or benchmark evidence.

1. C.D. Meyer, D.S. Balsara, T.D. Aslam, *A second-order accurate Super TimeStepping formulation for anisotropic thermal conduction* (2012), [doi:10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.20744.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.20744.x). Primary RKL2 construction and parabolic application. Our weighted graph proof and entrywise-positivity counterexample state their own assumptions; they are not a blanket refutation of every positivity claim in this paper.
2. Same authors, *A stabilized Runge–Kutta–Legendre method for explicit super-time-stepping of parabolic and mixed equations*, [doi:10.1016/j.jcp.2013.08.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2013.08.021). Background on stabilized Legendre integration.
3. A. Abdulle, *Fourth Order Chebyshev Methods with Recurrence Relation*, [doi:10.1137/S1064827500379549](https://doi.org/10.1137/S1064827500379549). Establishes that orthogonal-polynomial stabilization is an existing numerical family, not a novelty claim here.
4. A. Abdulle, C.-E. Bréhier, G. Vilmart, *Convergence analysis of explicit stabilized integrators for parabolic semilinear stochastic PDEs*, [arXiv:2102.03209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.03209). Explains spectral assumptions, quadratic stability interval and need for damping. Our implementation does not claim to reproduce that stochastic method.
5. M. Hochbruck, A. Ostermann, *Exponential Runge-Kutta methods for parabolic problems*, [author manuscript](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000042292/3168261). Supports separating classical order from stiff convergence, boundary regularity and equilibrium preservation.
6. M. Hochbruck, A. Ostermann, J. Schweitzer, *Exponential Rosenbrock-Type Methods*, [doi:10.1137/080717717](https://doi.org/10.1137/080717717). Relevant exponential methods use Jacobian linearization; they are not evidence that the present generic nonlinear solver is fully derivative-free.
7. M. Caliari, F. Cassini, L. Einkemmer, A. Ostermann, *Accelerating Exponential Integrators to Efficiently Solve Semilinear Advection-Diffusion-Reaction Equations*, [arXiv:2303.15861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15861). Supports extracting a tractable constant-coefficient operator and evaluating the remaining coupling separately in2D/3D.
