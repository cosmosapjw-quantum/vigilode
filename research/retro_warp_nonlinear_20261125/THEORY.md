R5. Total-degree quotient A_(r,D)=R[xi_1,...,xi_r]/m^(D+1), m=(xi_1,...,xi_r). dim C(r+D,r). Delta has zero constant. B=DF(P) is block lower bidiagonal with D_i=I-theta*h*J_i and subdiagonal -E_i=-(I+(1-theta)*h*J_i), J_i evaluated at forecast stage. D_i strongly monotone and invertible. Write H=B Delta+sum xi_a R_a+N_P(Delta). N_P has degree>=2. Degree alpha coefficient satisfies B X_alpha=-[xi^alpha](sum xi_a R_a+N_P(Delta)); lower positive grades only, hence unique formal solution inductively. At each step X_alpha,i=D_i^-1(E_i X_alpha,i-1+S_alpha,i), X_alpha,0=0, using cached factor for each time node. For term beta v(v^T y)^d, nonlinear residual higher terms are +h beta v sum_{k=2}^d binom(d,k)s^(d-k) u^k with s=v^T P_stage and u=v^T Delta_stage. Use multiplication pairs of positive degree to compute powers. Factorization count N per center, not one across all time. Actual implementation causal coefficient sweep, not parallel all-time solve; FFT candidate not used because Round05 simple recurrence costs favor sequential. Could use temporal preconditioner only when structure and measured cost justify it.

Diagonal collapse: D(xi_a)=t descends to quotient since all monomials total degree D+1 map to t^(D+1). D is algebra homomorphism, commutes with F and constant B. Formal solution unique, so X_diag,k=sum_{|alpha|=k} X_alpha exactly in quotient. r=3,D=4 has 35 coefficients vs5; nonconstant solve ratio34/4=8.5. D=6 ratio83/6. At off-diagonal targets full packet retains separate parameter response; collapse cannot recover independent mixed derivatives. At xi=ones, evaluation is NOT an algebra homomorphism from truncated quotient into R (nilpotents must map to zero), so lifting alone does not certify finite-parameter root. Candidate evaluated as represented polynomial; certify original and bounded re-centering, no arbitrary convergence promise. For any analytic F with B invertible, formal lifting valid, analytic continuation to ones may fail. Negative case retained.
