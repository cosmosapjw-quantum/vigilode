## Why this integration is needed

PR90–93 contain complementary execution, derivative-free causal/homotopy,
rounding and polynomial results. Their local certificates have different
targets and error anchors, so combining them without an explicit physical
contract can produce a false global bound or lose paid session state.

## What this PR adds

- A common-flow error contract, the two valid discrete/flow anchors and an exact
  counterexample to mixing them; norm, representation and clock ownership.
- A constructive quotient compiler for the whole declared affine raw-U stage
  system. Endpoint-only evaluation needs at most d−1 full-space chart actions
  after scalar preparation. Near-relation, stored-coefficient, base-RHS and
  nonlinear defects retain an original-stage residual bound.
- Precision-aware right-defect refinement, including the derived floor,
  least sufficient correction integer and the theta=0 equality boundary.
- Continuation-state-aware budget allocation and complete winning-session
  retention, with original-target admission and charged failure work.
- A Korean synthesis, a rendered 10-page REVTeX report, exact-source evidence,
  and an executable plan/handoff for a **later separate validation PR targeting
  main**. The plan covers all PR90–93 changes: 73 paths, 18 gate groups and ten
  interactions while retaining the inherited 23-node/34-edge/eight-suite plan.
