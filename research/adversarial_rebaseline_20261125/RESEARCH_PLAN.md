## M5: nonautonomous / arbitrary aperiodic whole-window solver
Use M3 prepared linear systems and M4 ordered coefficients. General actual ODE f(t,y), use frozen trajectory Jacobian in time-ordered Green, q robust from source perturbation bounds or explicit unknown; no assumed future BC, no autonomous substitution or unproved derivative recovery. Existing retro_temporal, retro_causal, retro_nonlinear, nonnormal_certificate, causal_budget, ordered Picard and native session.
