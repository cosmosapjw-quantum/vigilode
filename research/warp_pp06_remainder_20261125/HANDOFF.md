## Math-to-code
PP06 stores Fourier-polynomial coefficients (k,j) for exp(i k omega h tau) tau^j. Harmonic conjugation (k,j)->(-k,j), complex conjugate; formal degree is NOT physical time. Original nonlinear product has band <= 3K+S, degree <=3P+Q. Do not truncate formal degrees before primitive: nonzero harmonic primitive lowers degree. Keep full noncyclic harmonic padding, full output support. With 2N the full padded formal length, F=F0+t^N F1, F mod(t^N-1)=F0+F1, F mod(t^N+1)=F0-F1. Recover F0,F1 by half sum/difference. Inputs with degree >=N folded using +/- carry, not rejected or dropped. Twist rho=exp(i pi/N) on coefficients for negacyclic sector, inverse twist after DFT. With negative-sign forward DFT, conjugate coefficient path spectrum equals conjugate of original spectrum at (u,-v) untwisted or (u,1-v) twisted. Reuse a,b,phase transforms, no extra transforms for conjugate channels. Full and CRT share precomputed radix-2 twiddles and permutation plans. This CRT identity is a computational instance of the prior WARP carry/reconstruction idea; it does not implement generic geometric homotopy, arbitrary monic modulus or root extraction.

New public opt-in submodule fourier_path_candidate::warp. Full uses one grid; Crt uses two half-width sectors unless full formal length=1. Reject nonfinite model/inputs/output and invalid sigma/negative step; checked size arithmetic, cap 2^20 logical full grid points, checked cumulative counters. Work counters record successful calls, not partial failed work. Cplx binary64 computations approximate, no claim of rigorous FFT error certificate; original PP05 noncyclic interval certificate, primitive, commit and driver are unchanged. No new dependency. No arbitrary coefficient pruning. Maximum single grid is not measured peak RSS; full logical grid capacity and information not halved by CRT. Original PP06 G3 FAIL retained.
