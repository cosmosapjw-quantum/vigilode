# G4-S0 literature source map

- Linda Petzold, “Automatic Selection of Methods for Solving Stiff and Nonstiff Systems of Ordinary Differential Equations,” SIAM J. Sci. Stat. Comput. 4 (1983), DOI 10.1137/0904010. Relevance: end-of-step information, automatic switching, low selection overhead.
- PETSc `TSAdaptCandidateAdd` / `TSAdaptCandidatesGet`. Relevance: candidate order, stage order, stability coefficient, and relative cost as separate method metadata.
- P. J. Deka and L. Einkemmer, “Efficient adaptive step size control for exponential integrators,” arXiv:2102.02524 / published version. Relevance: cost per unit simulated time rather than largest accuracy-admissible step.
