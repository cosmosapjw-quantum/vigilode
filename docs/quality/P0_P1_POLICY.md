# VigilODE P0/P1 Execution Policy

## P0

P0 includes silent scientific/trajectory change, weakened true-residual authority, uncounted operator work, omitted failures, wrong equation/sign/tolerance/family identity, fabricated or rebound evidence, and canonical-data mutation.

## P1

P1 includes incomplete work reported as complete, hidden telemetry overhead, wrong initial-guess provenance, broken recycle rollback, tautological geometry, norm telemetry misrepresented as sign validation, claim inflation, and GitHub–Atlassian identity drift.

## Required guard

Every foreseeable P0/P1 must have at least one:

1. executable regression or negative test;
2. mechanically checked invariant/assertion;
3. fail-closed STOP when mechanical verification is impossible.

## Agent rules

- Do not ask user questions when repository authority can resolve them.
- Do not guess across a specification boundary.
- Do not rewrite tests, tolerances, families, or reference outputs to obtain green.
- Do not suppress failed stages/cells/checks.
- Do not claim unexecuted work.
- One fresh-context review and one final differential audit are sufficient unless a new P0/P1 failure class is found.
